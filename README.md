## 📊 Dashboards
- **Sales Dashboard**: KPIs (Total Sales, Orders, Customers), category/store breakdown, monthly trends.
- **Inventory Dashboard**: Stock levels, low inventory alerts, product availability by store.
- **Customer & Staff Dashboard**: Top customers, staff performance, regional order distribution.

---

## ⚙️ Technical Implementation
- **SQL Views**: Created for KPIs (sales, inventory, staff, customers).
- **DAX Measures**: Defined for Power BI (Total Sales, Monthly Sales, Stock Levels, Customer Sales, Staff Sales).
- **Power BI Features**: Drill‑down, slicers, conditional formatting, interactive maps.

---

## 📈 Business Insights
- Sales concentrated in one store & category → risk of over‑dependence.
- Inventory imbalances → need redistribution & smarter replenishment.
- Customer loyalty driven by repeat buyers.
- Staff performance varies → opportunities for recognition & training.
- Regional concentration in CA, TX, NY → untapped markets elsewhere.
